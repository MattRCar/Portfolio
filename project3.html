Appine animations

Project Overview

This project demonstrates various animations implemented in the Appine application. This was a test site for an assignment where we recreated from the ground up with given assets and then implemented various animations to enhance the user experience.

Back to Projects View Live Project

Project Development & Process

Planning & Design

I started by looking at the orginal site and breaking into down into smaller components to better understand its structure and functionality. This helped me plan how to rebuild the site while maintaining its original functionality and improving its design.

Development & Implementation

When developing the site, I focused on accurately recreating the original layout and functionality. I implemented various animations to enhance the user experience and make the interface more engaging. I paid close attention to detail and continuously tested the animations to ensure they worked smoothly and enhanced the overall user experience. For the animations I went to public sites such as Animate.css and other animation libraries to find suitable effects and inspiration.

Challenges & Solutions

Challenges I faced were trying to get exact positions and colors correct. I overcame these by carefully inspecting the original site and making iterative adjustments to match the design accurately. I also got feedback from other students and my professor to make sure I understand all that is being given out and making sure each part was implemented correctly.

Final Outcome & Reflection

The final outcome was a faithful recreation of the original site with enhanced animations that improved the overall user experience. This project taught me the importance of attention to detail and iterative development in achieving a polished final product.
