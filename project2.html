Digital Reflection of Korea

Project Overview

A site that is showcasing my journey and experiences in Korea.

It includes blog posts, photos, and reflections on my time in Korea. from each day to week it goes over everything i have experienced and learned to help me grow as a person. I am very proud of the content I have created and the memories I have captured with friends and fellow students.

Project Development & Process

Planning & Design

I started to plan this full project out even before stepping foot in Korea. I outlined the structure of the website, the types of content I wanted to include, and how I would organize my reflections and media to best capture my experiences. I wanted to be able to show what I saw and learned there but in a fun and engaging way.

Development & Implementation

I developed the website using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, ensuring that it was responsive and visually appealing. I incorporated various multimedia elements, such as photos and videos, to enhance the storytelling aspect of the site. I also implemented a blog section where I could regularly post updates and reflections on my experiences in Korea.

Challenges & Solutions

One of the main challenges was managing and organizing a large amount of content, including text, images, and videos. I overcame this by creating a clear content structure and using categories and tags to organize the blog posts. Another challenge was ensuring that the website was visually engaging and user-friendly, which I addressed through careful design and user testing. It was also fairly difficult to chose which content to prioritize and how to present it effectively.

Final Outcome & Reflection

The final website successfully captured my experiences in Korea, providing an engaging and organized platform for sharing my reflections and media. This project taught me valuable lessons in content management, responsive design, and the importance of balancing visual appeal with usability.
