History of Video Games

A digital publication concept exploring the evolution of video games, gaming companies, and influential titles.

Project Overview

A digital publication design project created for DMD 2470.

History of Video Games is a digital publication concept focused on exploring the history of gaming through featured articles, gaming companies, and influential video game titles.

The project includes a homepage, a PlayStation article collection, and a feature article exploring the legacy of Final Fantasy VII.

I developed initial wireframes before creating the final visual designs, experimenting with typography, color, imagery, and content organization.

Technologies Used

Design Process & Reflection

Planning & Wireframes

I started by creating wireframes to establish the overall structure of the publication, including its homepage, article categories, and individual article layouts. I wanted to build this site based on retro video game aesthetics, and my personal favorite game Final Fantasy influenced a lot of the design choices.

Visual Design

For the completed design, I explored a darker color palette with bright accents inspired by video game aesthetics. I incorporated game imagery, typography, and visual sections to give the publication a stronger identity. When it came to the articles, I focused on readability and visual hierarchy to ensure a pleasant reading experience, but also that matched the aesthetic of the orginal videogame.

Final Outcome & Reflection

This project gave me an opportunity to explore digital editorial design and understand how typography, layout, imagery, and visual hierarchy work together to present information. It also allowed me to experiment with incorporating video game aesthetics into a digital publication. It was a very interesting and enjoyable project that allowed a lot of creative exploration.
